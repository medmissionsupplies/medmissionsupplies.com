Privacy and site measurement

Daily website and article totals

From October 22, 2026, we also count page views, visits within a browser tab, article opens, link and share clicks, and engaged reading across public pages. Engaged reading means at least 60 seconds in a focused, visible page plus an interaction. A click is not proof of an inquiry, application, install, or purchase.

Only daily totals by public page, event and broad source category are retained for 90 days. We do not keep individual browsing histories, visitor identifiers, search text, full referring URLs, answers, scores, phone numbers or email addresses in these counters. A source category and time are kept in this tab for up to 30 minutes of inactivity. Random per-event receipts expire after three hours and only prevent duplicate delivery; they do not join a person's events. Internal testing is counted separately. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable optional measurement. Totals stay on our server and are not sent to advertising platforms.

Form contents are sent only to the existing inquiry service for responding to your request, separate from these counters.
