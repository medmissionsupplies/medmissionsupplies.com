Earlier evidence and measurement limits

Bible’s earlier article counters

Cumulative opens have no date window and did not reliably exclude staff, previews or bots. They are preserved and never added to the new daily totals.

How staff and testing are excluded

Open the relevant website using one of these links once in each testing browser. The visible Internal test mode badge confirms exclusion. It stays on that browser until you choose Turn off. Use the links for all future QA.

Known QA links and WebDriver also use the test bucket. We cannot identify an unmarked staff browser without tracking a person; such visits may enter visitor totals. Historical testing cannot be reliably removed retroactively.

Definitions, privacy and known gaps

Traffic

Page views and 30-minute browser-tab visits are not unique humans. No-JS, privacy signals, ad blockers and storage restrictions can cause undercounting. Obvious bots are filtered, but a bot acting like a browser can still count.

Useful outcomes

MMS acceptance means the form service accepted a request, not that staff received a qualified lead. New profile saves are separated from existing-profile loads. App Store clicks do not establish installs, activation or retention.

Data handling

Daily totals stay on the existing server for 90 days. Reporting has no public endpoint. Per-event retry hashes expire after three hours and do not connect a visitor’s steps. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable optional counting.
